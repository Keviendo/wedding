Questions

What should I wear?
Formal or semi-formal. Dresses, tuxedos, dress shirts, and button-ups are all fine. We kindly ask that you not wear white.
What if I'm running late?
Unfortunately, we won't be able to bring you in. The chapel opens its doors at 1:00 pm, and they stay closed until the ceremony ends at 1:30 pm. Please plan to arrive by 12:45 pm. You're welcome to join us for photos outside the chapel afterward.
Is the ceremony indoors or outdoors?
Indoors, in the chapel. We'll take photos outside afterward, and December afternoons in Riverside can be cool, so bring a light jacket.
Why is it important to RSVP?
The chapel holds 40 guests, and that count includes children and photographers. Your reply helps us keep a seat for everyone.
Can we throw petals or rice?
Not inside. The chapel doesn't allow anything to be thrown or dropped on the aisle.
Can I take photos or video?
Photos are welcome during the ceremony. We kindly ask that you not record video, as we'd like our vows to remain a private moment. The chapel allows photos inside only during our ceremony time; outside, anytime. Other couples may be taking photos too, so please give them room.
Are there restrooms?
There's a restroom inside the chapel. It's open only during our ceremony time.
Can I bring a guest?
Not to the ceremony. The chapel holds only 40 guests, so the ceremony is for the people named on your invitation. Additional guests are welcome at the reception at 2:30 pm; just include them in your RSVP.
